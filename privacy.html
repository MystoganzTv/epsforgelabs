Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EP Spaangberg Inc., operating as EPS Forge Labs ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses and protects information when you use this website (the "Services"). Each of our apps (RBT Genius, Bookliz, Foldiq, OnRoad Books and GoHostlyx) has its own privacy policy, published on the app's website or App Store page, which applies when you use that app.

1. Information we collect

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or advertising trackers. The only information involved is:

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically processes basic request data, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services.
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse or technical issues.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Services (such as hosting, analytics or payment processing), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and of EPS Forge Labs.

4. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the Services and for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.
